# Research source ledger

- **SRC-001** [Disney Animation — Animation](https://disneyanimation.com/process/animation/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Actor intention, timing, posing, weight, staging, appeal and follow-through. Limitation: general principles; not quantitative footfall thresholds.
- **SRC-002** [Disney Animation — Rigging](https://disneyanimation.com/process/rigging/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Rigs as controls enabling character performance. Limitation: rig design is shot-/character-dependent.
- **SRC-003** [Disney Animation — Technical Animation](https://disneyanimation.com/process/technical-animation/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Simulation and technical animation must remain art directed and physically plausible. Limitation: applies to hair/cloth and dynamic elements.
- **SRC-004** [The Science Behind Pixar — Rigging](https://sciencebehindpixar.org/pipeline/rigging) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Rig designed around story performance requirements. Limitation: not an endorsed code implementation.
- **SRC-005** [Autodesk Maya — Character Animation](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/ENU/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-0D0DCBE5-01BA-4AA2-BC4D-85C3285933AD.htm) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Skeleton, IK handles, binding, deformers and constraints. Limitation: Maya-specific capabilities; not automatically 2D canvas.
- **SRC-006** [Autodesk Maya — Posing Skeletons](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2027/ENU/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-390788DB-527D-4EE5-A8DC-B99BE2D4861F.htm) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: FK and IK posing fundamentals. Limitation: correct implementation requires validation.
- **SRC-007** [Autodesk Maya — Using Skeletons](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2023/ENU/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-360A07B4-9C74-4996-92EC-3BF4E9045DA3.htm) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: FK, IK and IK/FK blending considerations. Limitation: joint/control constraints vary by body plan.
- **SRC-008** [Autodesk Maya — IK posing tutorial](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2020/ENU/Maya-GettingStarted/files/GUID-088F7E4F-6D51-49BE-BAA1-E78417F316E4.htm) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Knee/elbow bend stability, foot/knee/hip IK behavior. Limitation: not evidence of finished rig quality.
- **SRC-009** [Blender Manual — IK Constraint](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/animation/constraints/tracking/ik_solver.html) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: IK target, chain behavior, and constraints. Limitation: solver setup depends on application.
- **SRC-010** [Monmouth University — Walk Cycle](https://animation.monmouth.edu/instruct/animation/walk-cycle/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Contact, passing, timing concepts in walk cycles. Limitation: teaching reference, not species-specific biomechanics.
- **SRC-011** [RMCAD — Animating Non-Human Characters](https://www.rmcad.edu/blog/animating-non-human-characters-challenges-and-techniques/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Body plan, mass distribution, multiple-limb gait and deformation considerations. Limitation: educational overview, not primary kinematic measurements.
- **SRC-012** [RMCAD — Animating Creatures and Animals](https://www.rmcad.edu/blog/animating-creatures-animals-anatomy-gait-and-personality/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Animal-specific observational reference for gait, personality, rhythm. Limitation: educational overview; confirm against species video.
- **SRC-013** [Pixar in a Box — Rigging](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/pixar-rigging) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Rigging and accessible instructional examples. Limitation: lessons are not a universal rig recipe.
- **SRC-014** [Disney Animation — Modeling](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/modeling/) — **studied_public_page**. Supports: Visual design supports personality and future rigging needs. Limitation: early-stage design concern.
- **SRC-015** [User-supplied short-form structure screenshot](90_ARCHIVE/V7/14_STORY_STRUCTURE/REF-STORY-0001.md) — **provided_visual_reference**.
