# Module 03 — Sound action semantics

Input: event graph and scene/acoustic context. Identify sound source and material, source visibility, in-world or score, point of audition, distance/occlusion/environment, attack/body/decay, role (information/texture/emotion/transition), and relationship to visual contact frames. Output a spotting sheet including event-id, trigger frame, tentative timbre layers, priority in mix, potential silence, and how audience meaning changes with context.

Do not invent real audio samples or license rights. Distinguish `THUD` as script shorthand from an actual Foley sample. If no sound artifact exists, mark planned/unverified. Verify mono/phone playback when available; do not assert LUFS from a description.
