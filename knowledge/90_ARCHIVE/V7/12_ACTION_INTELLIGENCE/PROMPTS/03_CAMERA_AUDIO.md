# Module 03 — Camera and audio director

**Input:** causal blocking and what character/audience know per frame.

For each beat choose camera goal, shot size, height, focal/perspective characteristics, screen axis, camera movement, cut rationale and visual attention target. In parallel create audio event list identifying emitter, location, onset, source reality, Foley material, acoustic perspective, mix priority and whether sound intentionally leads image. Explain why each selection serves the story. Reject decorative moves and generic impacts not justified by action.
