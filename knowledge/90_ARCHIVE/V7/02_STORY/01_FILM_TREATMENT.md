# DINO//DISASTER — Episode 01: WRONG EXIT

**Genre:** stylized visual comedy and adventure with family-centered emotional reversal. **Default narrative:** no spoken dialogue or narrator; sound/acting convey everything. **Length:** planning target 80s, with benchmark first 9s.

## Logline

A curious traveler steps through a portal into prehistory and saves a falling dinosaur egg—only for its mother to mistake him for a thief. The egg hatches during their chase, reconciliation follows, and an affectionate baby dinosaur turns the escape home into an even bigger problem.

## Theme and emotional promise

Looks can mislead; a frightening stranger and a compassionate stranger can interpret the same event differently. The audience begins aligned with human fear, then understands the mother's protectiveness, then shares affection for the newborn. Finale flips warmth into comic chaos without negating the reunion.

## Treatment, eight causally connected sequences

**1. Arrival / Wonder (0–9).** Quiet prehistoric forest. A dimensional aperture opens at ground level, bending paper foliage outward. The traveler's face appears within the glowing opening, considering whether to step into the unfamiliar world. He crosses one leg, plants his leading foot on real grass, transfers his weight and clears his trailing leg. He regains balance, takes in giant plants and notices the world is enormous. The portal **remains open** behind him.

**2. Rescue / Responsibility (9–19).** A distant vibration unsettles a nest resting atop a rock ledge. An egg trembles, shifts and begins rolling toward a drop. The traveler recognizes the danger. With an impulsive, awkward leap, he catches it in both hands before it strikes a rock. He is relieved—until a much stronger footstep arrives from outside his field of view.

**3. Misunderstanding / Threat (19–30).** A gigantic dinosaur foot lands in frame. The mother approaches from deeper east, checks her now-empty nest, and sees a tiny stranger holding her egg. Her body freezes, pupils narrow, chest draws breath, and she issues a protective roar. The human tries to show what happened but, hearing the roar, recoils.

**4. Chase / Protection (30–46).** The human tries to set the egg down safely, but the mother advances too fast. He clutches it against his chest and flees west toward the portal. The mother gives chase, her pace building despite mass. He sees a low branch early enough to duck, while the mother breaks through. A root almost trips him; he recovers without dropping the egg. The mother is now much closer.

**5. Hatching / Reinterpretation (46–56).** A tiny crack cuts through the panic. The human hears it, looks down and brakes. As the shell opens, a baby dinosaur peeks out and chirps. The mother hears the sound and skids to a halt, her tail still moving after her legs stop. Human and mother focus on the same newborn. The conflict's meaning changes instantly.

**6. Reunion / Trust (56–66).** The traveler carefully crouches, lowers the baby until its feet support it, then releases. The newborn takes tentative steps to its mother, who nuzzles it and shifts from protective aggression to tenderness. The mother looks back at the human and gives a soft exhale of acceptance; he offers a timid wave. The baby responds to his wave and gives him a distinct friendly chirp—**a setup for its later choice**.

**7. Follow / Unseen Consequence (66–74).** Human leaves for the portal, where he passes completely into the aperture. Baby watches closely, eyes moving between the traveler and mother. Mother turns briefly toward the remains of the shell/nest, checking the source of earlier disturbance. Baby chooses to follow its rescuer, chirps and crosses the same opening. Mother turns at the sound, sees its departure and rushes to the threshold.

**8. Wrong Exit / Cliffhanger (74–80).** On the modern apartment side, the human emerges into a familiar room; baby tumbles out after him, startling him. As they face each other, a low pressure sound swells from the open portal. Its flexible rim stretches outward. Mother's enormous snout pushes through, followed by one curious eye. The human slowly raises his gaze. Smash cut to black on a comedy sting.

## Core character arcs

- **Human:** uncertain visitor → helpful impulsive rescuer → frightened protector → surprised caregiver → confident returner → comic victim of friendship.
- **Mother:** searching protector → mistaken accusation → forceful pursuit → surprised listener → relieved affectionate parent → protective pursuit again (not villainous aggression).
- **Baby:** endangered egg → vulnerable hatchling → bonded child → intentional follower whose attachment creates complication.

## Why each scene must exist

1 shows actual otherworld crossing; 2 causes misunderstanding; 3 creates conflict; 4 amplifies stakes while protecting egg; 5 reframes conflict; 6 resolves it; 7 plants new complication; 8 pays it off. If a future revision cuts one, replace its causal function elsewhere rather than leaving a gap.

## Silent-playback comprehension questions

A first-time viewer should be able to answer: Where did human come from? Why did he pick up egg? Why is mother angry? Why does human keep egg while running? Why do both stop? What changes the mother's attitude? Why does baby follow? Why does mother enter the portal? Any ambiguous answer is a story or direction defect, not a caption-writing problem.
