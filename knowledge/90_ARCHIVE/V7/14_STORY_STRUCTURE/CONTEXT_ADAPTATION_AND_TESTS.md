# When to use a short-form hook–open-loop structure

## Required context questionnaire (answer before story-template selection)

- **Format:** factual explainer, character-led fiction, comedy, silent short, documentary, news, promo, or tutorial?
- **Promise:** what is the honest core question or emotional payoff?
- **Audience information:** what does the viewer know that the character does not, and vice versa?
- **Duration / platform:** 15–30s vs 80s vs long-form; mobile safe areas, audience expectation.
- **Energy:** suspense, wonder, warmth, humor, contemplation. Fast cuts are not required for every beat.
- **Visual causality:** where do we *see* the event rather than merely hear or read it?
- **Sound:** diegetic event, score, silence, narration and their source/perspective.
- **Resolution:** which questions are answered and which one, if any, is carried to next episode?
- **CTA choice:** necessary for social publishing? intrusive in fiction? omit when harmful to payoff.

## Application matrix

| Context | Use of eight-beat reference | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| 25s factual short | Good candidate, test rather than assume | Honest hook, early fact, one loop, clear answer, optional CTA |
| 80s DINO fiction | *Conceptual* use only | Opening visual question, escalating misunderstandings, egg reveal, reunion, baby follows, final cliffhanger. Keep emotional pauses; no mandatory promotional CTA |
| Somber documentary | Conditional | Avoid repetitive curiosity gaps or unsupported sensationalism; prefer clear chronology and evidence |
| Calm landscape animation | Usually poor literal fit | Wonder and observation may be better than multiple open loops |
| Instructional motion design | Sometimes helpful | Hook names problem; body demonstrates steps; loops must not withhold safety-critical instructions |

## Experiment card template

`EXP-ID`, `genre`, `duration`, `audience knowledge`, `viewer promise`, `story outline`, `loop introduction`, `payoff`, `camera`, `motion`, `sound`, `fidelity`, `control variant`, `viewer comprehension protocol`, `metrics`, `confounders`, `result`, `evidence path`, `status`.

**Rule:** No framework gets a 'universal' or 'validated for retention' tag without direct multi-context evaluation. The screenshot itself is not validation of those claims.
