# PROMPT 04 — Layout, camera, cinematography and visual attention

## Copy into agent

> Act as cinematographer and layout director. Read the story state, `01_RESEARCH/01_VISUAL_CAMERA_DIRECTION.md` and `01_RESEARCH/05_CONTEXT_COUPLING.md`. For EACH shot first state the audience's information need and the character's current knowledge. Then choose shot size, camera height/angle, actor staging, look room, axis side, focal hierarchy, start/end camera coordinates, allowed parallax, timing and cut intention. A camera move must reveal, follow, establish scale, empathize or reframe; otherwise lock it off. Protect the main physical event in frame: complete portal crossing, egg catch, baby handoff, branch contact. Use a consistent world-to-camera transform and actor-agnostic camera path. Provide beginning/middle/end framing thumbnails and eye-trace notes across edits. Specify key light direction, portal emission, paper shadows, and color-script state by beat. Show a grayscale/thumb-size test before effects polish. Do not invent orbital views of single-facing 2.5D puppets.

## Output shot-card schema

`shot_id, seconds/frame_range, viewer_question, character_known_information, primary_actor_action, proposed frame, axis side, focal item, camera purpose, start/end pose, lighting, screen-direction continuity, sound POV, cut/bridge, reject conditions`.

## Explicit no-go

Fast zoom to hide a missing human foot plant; camera shake on every dinosaur step regardless of proximity; portal cropped such that body crossing cannot be verified; action axis flip with no establishing geography; close-ups used instead of decisive physical contact evidence; excessive parallax layer swimming.
