# Five-gate animation acceptance standard (separate from file validation)

**Never approve a scene on the basis of file existence or perfect encoder logs alone.** A video can be technically valid while its acting, logic and emotion fail, as observed in the original 5-second DINO studies.

## Gate 1 — story and action completion

- Source event or emotional trigger visible/audible, in correct order.
- Character perception/intention clear before major decision.
- Decisive action completed visibly: portal crossing, catch, collision, hatch, release, recognition.
- Consequences are seen and actor states updated; there is a recover/settle beat.
- No missing physical event concealed by a cut, caption, flare or sound.
- Silent first-time viewer can answer who did what, why, where, and what changed.

## Gate 2 — mechanics, contact and acting

- Rig pivots, body ratios, stance contacts and weight transfer credible.
- Root position vs world/camera coordinate separation correct.
- Gaze leads turns; whole body responds to acceleration/roar/impact.
- Foot locked to world during stance; no visible skating.
- Egg/child attachment and release states physically coherent.
- Dinosaur mass shows tail and torso inertia; baby has small unstable steps.
- Displacement, anticipation and follow-through follow dramatic context, not generic identical easing.

## Gate 3 — camera, design and editing

- Shot size/angle/move purpose written and manifested in real picture.
- Screen direction/180° axis, eyelines, matched action, eye-trace and geography coherent or deliberate exception justified.
- Camera keeps critical interactions in view and does not hide mistakes.
- Important silhouettes separate; grain, cutouts, shadows, light direction and scale consistent.
- Appropriate visual attention hierarchy and mobile-safe framing.
- No broken 2.5D parallax, texture swimming, unwanted zoom jitter or sudden cropping.

## Gate 4 — audio and music

- All sounds have provenance, source or intentional psychological function.
- Contact-locked Foley on actual event frames, not arbitrary rhythmic loop.
- Offscreen anticipatory audio and sound bridges are deliberate and clear.
- Ambience matches forest/apartment and persists through picture cuts.
- Mother breath, roar, footsteps, baby chirp and shell crack convey size/emotion.
- Music supports emotional changes and retains quiet; key Foley audible on phone.
- Final export contains sound, appropriate stereo/mono consistency, no clipping, measured levels and listened playback.

## Gate 5 — technical, export and reproducibility

- Correct dimensions, 30fps progressive, duration/frame count per approved plan, seekable stream.
- Deterministic source frames; exact version/dependency manifest; portable regenerate steps.
- Color/Rec.709 output fidelity, no invisible transparency/alpha flattening errors.
- Actual MP4 audio/video sync, codec and sample rate checked, no double lossy pass without reason.
- Images/scene/media referenced by real paths; license/asset provenance preserved.
- Contact sheet, consecutive frames, defects and evidence report saved.

## Severity rubric

- **BLOCKER:** critical story action absent; portal mis-exit; character/object teleport at decisive event; corrupt/unavailable required render/audio, unlicensed necessary sound; severe logical contradiction.
- **MAJOR:** recurring planted-foot sliding, incorrect actor orientation, wrong reaction order, incoherent sound POV, hidden contact, significant edit geography defect, mobile-illegible focal content.
- **MINOR:** isolated visual polish, slight nonessential secondary timing, inconsequential texture variation.
- **SUGGESTION:** optional enhancement that does not change factual completion.

**Exit rule:** zero unresolved blockers AND zero unresolved majors for current phase; minor issues itemized and accepted by reviewer. Make no “perfect” quality claim. The same rubric applies to benchmark and final film, with scope appropriate to phase.

## Inspection modes

1. Normal-speed full-screen at native aspect ratio.
2. Silent viewing for causal comprehension.
3. Step-through adjacent frames around contacts, turns, cuts and occlusion.
4. Grayscale / thumbnail reduction for hierarchy and safe areas.
5. Foley+character+ambience only for physical sound.
6. Music-only and final mix for emotional arc, masking and unwanted loudness.
7. Phone speaker, headphones and mono collapse.
8. Media probe and source/regression checks.

An automated test can check structural problems and known event invariants; it cannot establish whether the animation conveys authentic emotion or correct story by itself. Human visual/auditory review is necessary.
