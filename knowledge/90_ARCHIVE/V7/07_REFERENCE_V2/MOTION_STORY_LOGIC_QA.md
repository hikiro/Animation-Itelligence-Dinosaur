# Story Logic and Character-Action QA

## Case: DINO//DISASTER, four 5-second studies (18 November 2026)

Source of evidence: `DINO_FOUR_STYLES_EDITABLE_PROJECT(1).zip`, 4 rendered clips, `motion.js`, `sound.py`.

### Overall judgment
The premise is readable (arrival in prehistoric world, dinosaur encounter, fright, chase), but the action-chain logic is 4/10. All four styles share essentially one action design, so narrative/physics defects recur.

### Five-second fixed action (example)

- 0.00–0.60: Portal opens on the ground, light spills onto the terrain.
- 0.60–1.30: Human plants one foot through opening, emerges with weight and looks around.
- 1.30–2.00: Heavy stomp and moving shadow; dinosaur's head turns toward human. Eye contact is established.
- 2.00–2.65: Dino takes a deliberate step, lowers head, opens mouth. Human notices and freezes.
- 2.65–3.35: Dino roars; human recoils, turns, shifts weight and pushes off.
- 3.35–4.50: Dino lunges and follows with contact-locked strides; it nearly catches the human.
- 4.50–5.00: A strong cliffhanger, e.g. closing portal catches human mid-leap or jaws narrowly miss, held long enough to read.

### Pass/fail questions for any future story-driven animation

1. **Geography:** Where is each character? Are contacts and occlusions correct?
2. **Motivation:** Why does a character choose each action? Is there a visible trigger?
3. **Perception:** Do eyes/head/body orient to the object that matters *before* interacting?
4. **Cause and effect:** Does reaction happen only after its cause, with understandable latency?
5. **Anticipation:** Does a major action have a believable preparatory pose/weight transfer?
6. **Physics:** Are direction, momentum, turning, foot contact, landing, impacts and secondary motions consistent?
7. **Relationship:** Do relative spacing and velocities match chasing, escaping, approaching or avoiding?
8. **Continuity:** Are objects still present when characters interact with them? Do exits lead somewhere?
9. **Emotion:** Do face, pose, gesture and sound express the same emotional state?
10. **Payoff:** Does the ending complete, reverse or meaningfully suspend the action?

**Acceptance gate:** Before stylistic polish, render a grayscale/silhouette animatic with audio muted. Ask a viewer to recount who did what, why, and what changed. If the chain is ambiguous, revise the staging before smoothing or decorating frames.
