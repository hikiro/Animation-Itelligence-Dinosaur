# LRN-0010: Action directions require a causal event graph

## Claim and scope

A visual verb is not self-verifying. The viewer must witness enough of stimulus, understanding, decision, preparation, decisive action and consequence to recover why the action occurred.

## Engineering translation

Sequence stage boundaries around the true event. Check source and target states; prefer contact-specific observations to expressions or decorative shake.

## Where not to overapply

Do not enforce a six-stage hold for reflexes or sound bridges. Valid offscreen events, surprise attacks and humor can compress awareness and anticipation.

## Observable acceptance test

Muted first-time viewing: independent observer correctly identifies cause, target, decisive contact and consequence. Example ACT-014/019/025.
