# LRN-0012: Camera and sound should share the audience information goal

## Claim and scope

A camera zoom, Foley thump or score accent is justified by what it reveals, conceals or makes emotionally salient. The actor and the viewer may have different knowledge.

## Engineering translation

For every major beat document actor knowledge, viewer knowledge, spatial source, framing, and mix priority. Use sound bridges deliberately; align contact Foley to emitter event.

## Where not to overapply

A sound intentionally leading a shot is not a timing mistake. Conventional continuity rules may be broken when the viewer can still follow the story.

## Observable acceptance test

For three context variations of a single stomp (fear, comedy, wonder), a silent viewer recount and sound listening check identify the intended genre with no temporal confusion.
