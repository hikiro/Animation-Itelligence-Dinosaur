## Interpretation by context — a nonbinding directing heuristic

| Beat | Candidate narrative purpose | Animated execution possibilities | Failure to detect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | Create an immediate question, surprise or compelling change | Cold open showing meaningful event or reaction, strong focus, grounded sound | Unrelated spectacle; action not understood |
| Lead | Give just enough premise and stakes to orient viewer | Clear geography/actor goal; establish a prop/object and cause | Viewer still doesn't know who wants what |
| Body 1 | Deliver first evidence/action, not filler | Demonstrate first causal event | Repeats hook with no development |
| Open loop 1 | Raise a specific unresolved question that later *pays off* | New implication, reveal withheld but foreshadowed | Artificial cliffhanger with no payoff |
| Body 2 | Develop/complicate established stakes | Counterexample, escalation, character decision | New unrelated information breaks narrative chain |
| Open loop 2 | Sustain an identifiable future question | New obstacle or choice that follows from Body 2 | Multiple untracked questions confuse viewer |
| Body 3 | Resolve or transform main question | Visible consequence; explain/reveal what happened | Essential setup never resolved |
| CTA | Context-dependent closing action or invitation | Subscribe/comment prompt if suitable, or a story-led next-episode tease | Sales language ruins emotional ending |

**Open-loop bookkeeping:** For every loop write `question`, `introduced_at`, `new_information`, `payoff_at`, `resolution`, `intentionally_unresolved?`. An unresolved end-of-episode loop must be intentional and unmistakable, not an accidental continuity error.

## Interaction with existing research

- AI-01 action semantics: a hook must still have a visible causal event; open loops depend on what the **viewer knows**, not just an arbitrary timeline.
- Camera: support visual focus and reveal; do not force zooms at every segment.
- Motion: preserve anticipation, physical contact and audience comprehension during high-energy openings.
- Audio: sound bridges and sonic questions can open loops, but sonic source/time/perspective must remain coherent.
- DINO film: the 80-second fiction story can adapt the *principle* of early question/complication/payoff; literal numbered bars and a promotional CTA are NOT automatically appropriate to a short narrative.
- FACT//SHIFT factual Shorts may benefit from a tighter hook→explanation→reveal→invitation structure, subject to factual correctness and viewer feedback.
