# Context-first direction and story semantics

**Never map one adjective to one effect without asking what it modifies.** "Heavy" may mean body mass, emotional silence, low-frequency sound, oppressive framing or impact weight; these are distinct variables.

## Required context tuple
- Who acts? Actor identity, personality, anatomy, physical capabilities, existing pose, current velocity and supports.
- What happened? Trigger, evidence the actor can perceive, and whether perception is plausible.
- What does the actor believe/want? Interpretation, motivation, goal, fear, relationship and stakes.
- What does the audience know? Subjective vs objective viewpoint, timing of reveals, previous and next shots.
- Where? Ground, props, obstacles, scale reference, light, weather, acoustic environment and camera side.
- In what genre/style? Realism, anime, paper-cut, pixel, stylized cartoon. Declare allowed exceptions.
- What change must be visible? Pre-state, contact/threshold, post-state and emotional consequence.

## Contextual algorithm
1. Parse literal words **without changing** them.
2. Resolve referents and roles: which "it"; who hears a sound; which hand holds the prop.
3. Label ambiguities and select interpretation from scene context. Escalate only when choice changes an essential outcome.
4. Generate causal beats, each bound to a readable pose or audio/camera information event.
5. Assign ground/object/world coordinates and state changes; exclude physically contradictory paths.
6. Select camera and sound according to what viewer needs to know now and when.
7. Produce technical plan and acceptance test, not just a list of effects.

## Same foot stomp, four readings
| Context | Character intention | Motion | Camera | Sound | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Threat | Human seeks escape | Freeze, turn to source, lean back, plant then flee | Low foot reveal then scale relationship | Deep grounded contact, breath, reduced score | Danger and source understood before flight |
| Comedy | Human overreacts | Delayed tiny stare then huge controlled hop | Hold wide enough for size contrast | Heavy thud → silence → tiny reaction cue | Pause and joke readable |
| Wonder | Human fascinated | Relax, lean in, track gaze upward | Reveal world/creature slowly | Warm resonant impact, ambience blooms | Awe rather than danger perceived |
| Tenderness | Mother approaches baby gently | Controlled foot plant avoiding baby, head lowers | Eye-level between mother and baby | Soft material compression and quiet chirp | Care clear from spatial relationships |
