# DINO//DISASTER — CINEMATIC DIRECTOR PACK v2.0

Read in this order:

1. `CINEMATOGRAPHY_AND_SOUND_RULEBOOK.md`: researched directing guidelines, sound grammar, postproduction QC and sources. Conventions are clearly distinguished from chosen project rules and delivery settings.
2. `DINO_DISASTER_CINEMATIC_DIRECTOR_MASTER_PROMPT_V2.md`: the complete **copy-ready prompt**. Use its entire contents as the governing instruction for an animation-capable coding agent.

**User-approved workflow:** Execute the 9-second portal-crossing benchmark first. Do not start polishing the entire 80-second episode until the entrance is visibly correct and the preview sound cues align with it. The animation is not rendered in this pack; it is a production specification, not a completed MP4.

**Fast review checklist:**
- Does the human visibly emerge from **inside** the portal in one uninterrupted shot, with no off-screen entrance?
- Does the opening physically meet the ground? Does near/far rim occlusion show threshold crossing?
- Are planted feet genuinely stable in world space and aligned with Foley?
- Do eyes/head/body react in the right causal order?
- Do shot cuts preserve portal-left, nest-right, mother-farther-right geography?
- Are visual style, color, lighting and parallax coherent?
- Is each key sound motivated, positioned and synchronized with an event?
- Do the story, emotion and punchline work without captions and on phone speakers?
- Was an actual frame and audio QC performed, with documented fixes rather than just a passing render command?

Sources are provided in the rulebook (Disney Animation, Pixar, Adobe, Frame.io, Sound on Sound, AES, BBC/EBU, YouTube and the three animation-principles links).
