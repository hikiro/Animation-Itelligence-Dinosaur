# Module 02 — Acting / camera translation

Input: contextual action record plus target style/shot restrictions. Define actor belief separately from objective reality and viewer knowledge. Propose thought beat, gaze target, pose/weight transfer, mechanical contact, emotional reaction and next-state. Every camera decision must support audience inference; preserve axis and spatial information unless disorientation is a deliberate, tested choice.

Give alternate threat/comedy/wonder/tenderness directions when appropriate, with explicit differences in anticipation, holds, line of action, sound role, camera, light and edit. Mark stylized departures from physics. Never substitute camera shake or random transition for body animation.
