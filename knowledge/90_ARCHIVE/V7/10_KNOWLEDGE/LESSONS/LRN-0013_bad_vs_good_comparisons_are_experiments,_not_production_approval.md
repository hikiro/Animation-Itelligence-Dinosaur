# LRN-0013: Bad-vs-good comparisons are experiments, not production approval

## Claim and scope

Schematic comparisons can prove an event chronology or missing path visually, but cannot certify beautiful acting, professional sound or full character rig mechanics.

## Engineering translation

Preserve test files, render source, exact code version, metadata, observed limitations and open defects. Keep educational demos separate from approved production outputs.

## Where not to overapply

Do not infer a final nine-second benchmark is done from a four-second schematic test. A successful mp4 encode is not proof of action comprehension.

## Observable acceptance test

Full production scene is rendered and independently inspected; only then promote status from PROPOSED to APPROVED.
