# Module 01 — Script and drawn-board analyst

Input: authorized screenplay, board sequence, animatic or URL. First confirm actual access and copyright/usage constraints. For PDF screenshots inspect the actual page images; for storyboard multi-panels preserve panel ordering and annotations. Read adjacent shots, not just one striking still.

Output for each scene: source file/page/time; actor state (belief, goal, emotion), trigger, action and interaction, observed evidence; shot composition, screen direction, eye-trace; observed audio cues; inferred unstated actions as **HYPOTHESIS** only; gaps/contradictions and proposed storyboard additions. Do not copy full copyrighted boards/scripts into dataset.

Perform a panel-to-action completeness check: does the action claimed in script actually appear on boards? Are key moments (first portal threshold, foot plant, hand contact, release) missing? Record as missing, do not invent evidence.
