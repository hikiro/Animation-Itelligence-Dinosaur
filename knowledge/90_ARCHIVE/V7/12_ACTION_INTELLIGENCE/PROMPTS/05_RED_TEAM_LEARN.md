# Module 05 — Independent reviewer, regression and learning

**Input:** exact render files, real event logs, quality gates.

Inspect silent story comprehension, body mechanics, contact/occlusion, camera continuity and audio perspective independently. Derive objective metrics where possible; do not confuse encoded MP4 validity with artistic quality. Try to disprove the claimed action rather than merely affirm it. For each defect, record timestamp, source and expected vs observed evidence, root cause, repair and rerender. Update `LEARNING_LOG.md`, `INDEX.md`, and cross-chat `STATUS.md` with exact evidence. A technical demo never approves the 270-frame DINO benchmark.
