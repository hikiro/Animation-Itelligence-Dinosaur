# Proposed experiment: one event across scales and bodyplans

**Hypothesis:** a single uniformly scaled animation will not convincingly communicate distinct scale and gait when body plan, mass distribution and style differ.

### Variables
- Actor: small hatchling, adult human-sized biped, giant fictional theropod; parallel horse-like quadruped test.
- Controlled constants: world grid, camera placement in comparison condition, ground material, contact event, seed, frame rate.
- Independent variables: relative size, stride cadence, joint rig, COM displacement, braking time, tail/body follow-through, sound perspective.
- Outputs: position/velocity, world-space foot contacts, joint angles, object intersections, frames and sound tracks.
- Comparisons: naive uniform scale vs anatomy/scale adapted; same creative intention across threat/comedy/wonder.

### Required checks
Support polygon/COM plausibility (not full dynamics proof); contact-foot drift; appendage intersections; gaze/pose read; independent silent viewers' perceived weight; listening test for mass and source. Report thresholds as empirical experiment settings, not universal laws.
