# Research taxonomy and search keys

Stable IDs: `SRC-####` (source); `LRN-####` (lesson); `PRE-####` (preset); `EXP-####` (experiment); `DEF-####` (observed defect); `GAP-####` (evidence gap); `DEC-####` (decision); `CONFLICT-####` (contradiction); `SES-####` (session).

Record context dimensions: `actor`, `anatomy`, `relative_scale`, `mass_cue`, `terrain`, `environment`, `material`, `emotion`, `intent`, `relationship`, `audience_knowledge`, `camera_axis`, `shot_size`, `lens_or_perspective`, `sound_source`, `sound_listener`, `style`, `preceding_beat`, `following_beat`, `evidence_level`, `failure`.

Suggested controlled topic tags: `perception, decision, anticipation, locomotion, support, contact, grab, handoff, collision, threshold, scale, creature, tail, wings, camera, staging, eye-trace, audio, Foley, music, silence, editing, typography, transition, expression, matte, rig, deform, QA`.

Every searchable entry should contain everyday synonyms as well as technical names: “sliding feet” → `contact/foot-lock`; “really heavy” → `mass-cue/inertia`; “character notices” → `perception/gaze`.
