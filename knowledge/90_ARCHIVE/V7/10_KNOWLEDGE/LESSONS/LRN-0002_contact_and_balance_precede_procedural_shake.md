# LRN-0002 — Contact and balance precede procedural shake

## Claim and scope
A shake accent can reinforce impact but cannot express applied force unless pose, limbs, balance and object trajectories visibly respond.

## Plain-language instruction → technical implementation
Plan contact location/time, direction of force, hips/torso/limb follow-through and ground support; then add controlled decaying shake or material accent.

## Where not to overapply / alternative interpretations
Subtle acting in restrained drama can be stronger than shaking; nonphysical comedy may use graphic impact but must keep clear causal state.

## Observable acceptance test
Inspect before/contact/after trio with FX disabled, then compare with FX enabled.

## Typical failure
Random wiggle applied to entire comp while receiver pose does not change.
