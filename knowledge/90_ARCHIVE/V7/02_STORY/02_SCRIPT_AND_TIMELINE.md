## Cinematic short script (visual action only)

### S01 FOREST — DAY — OPEN PORTAL

A bright forest stills. A cyan tear opens between ferns and reaches the ground. A small HUMAN'S HEAD peers out **within** the illuminated aperture, followed by one hand grasping the rim. One foot crosses from the other side, finds the grass and takes weight. The other leg clears; the human stands wholly outside, balances and marvels at huge plants. The portal remains shimmering behind him.

Audio: ambient forest bed; portal grows in volume and width; threshold shift; actual planted foot; small breath and curious vocalization. Camera: one uninterrupted legible full-body shot, stable.

### S02 NEST — A LITTLE LATER

Distant THUD. The human notices a speckled egg in a nest above a rocky drop. The nest trembles. Egg begins rolling. Human's eyes snap to it; he crouches, launches, catches the egg with both hands and stumbles under its momentum. Another, closer THUD changes relief into concern.

Audio: approaching distant footsteps, nest foliage, egg shell against twigs/air, hand catch, shoe scuff. Camera: eyeline from human toward egg then held medium/full-body action.

### S03 EASTERN CLEARING — CONTINUOUS

A huge mother dinosaur foot depresses the earth. She looks toward the nest: empty. Her eyes locate the egg in the human's hands. A tense breath expands her chest. He makes a nervous explanatory gesture. She ROARS. His head and shoulders recoil, knees compress, and his stance opens for flight.

Audio: weighty impact; inhale then roar; brief subjective reduction of forest detail during human's shock. Camera: scale reveal then clear mother-to-egg-to-human eyeline.

### S04 CHASE ROUTE — CONTINUOUS

The human begins setting down the egg but sees the mother advancing. He withdraws it, presses it against his chest and pivots west. The mother accelerates, closing the gap. He ducks a visible branch. She breaks through it. His foot catches a root, pitching his torso and forcing scrambling recovery steps. He never loses the egg.

Audio: contact-event footsteps, branch snap and tail of falling paper leaves, scuff, fast breath; score drives forward with contrast. Camera: mostly lateral tracking with continuous geography, show distance actually shrinking.

### S05 ROOT CLEARING — CONTINUOUS

CRACK. The human hears the small sound inside the egg. He brakes, looks down. The shell breaks along a visible line; a tiny head emerges, then a body. CHIRP. The approaching mother recognizes the new voice and slows hard, tail swinging after legs settle. Their attention converges.

Audio: score withdraws for tiny crack; chirp foreground, heavy braking scrape and tail rustle. Camera: ease to near-static, protect shell event then shared reaction.

### S06 NEAR MOTHER — CONTINUOUS

The human crouches, lowers the baby carefully, waits until tiny feet contact the ground, releases. The newborn waddles east into its mother’s nose; she nuzzles it and exhales gently. She looks at the human with softened posture. He timidly waves. Baby chirps directly at him, establishing mutual bond.

Audio: soft hand rustle and tiny feet, affectionate breath, warm score enters after recognition. Camera: baby-level medium; hold emotional recognition.

### S07 PORTAL CLEARING — CONTINUOUS

Human waves and goes west through the still-open portal, truly clearing its aperture. Baby notices his disappearance, glances at mother, who turns back toward broken shell, then dashes after human and physically crosses. Mother turns at the chirp, realizes baby has departed and lunges.

Audio: portal spectral bridge, baby foot/chirp, mother alarm after event. Camera: keep west-east geography readable in a wide shot.

### S08 HUMAN APARTMENT — INTERIOR — CONTINUOUS

The human emerges through the same aperture into a modest apartment. Baby hops through. The human turns in astonishment. A pressure note makes both look back. Portal rim stretches; giant snout pushes through; one eye arrives. Human looks slowly up. BLACK.

Audio: new room tone, landing and chirp, portal flex/tension, restrained comic impact and decay. Camera: medium on humans/portal then precisely staged reveal.

## Timing guardrail

Every script revision must preserve the entry and exit state of each scene. If a shot length changes, update **frame table, cue events, rig states, shot cards and camera cuts**; never adjust text without updating timing data.
