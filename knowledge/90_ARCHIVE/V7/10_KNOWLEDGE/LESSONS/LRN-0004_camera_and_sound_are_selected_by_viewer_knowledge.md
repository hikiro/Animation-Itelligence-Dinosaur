# LRN-0004 — Camera and sound are selected by viewer knowledge

## Claim and scope
Ask what viewer already knows, what should be perceived next, and whose perspective is active; camera and audio should jointly support that inference.

## Plain-language instruction → technical implementation
For unseen dinosaur: distant offscreen footfall can cause human gaze shift before reveal. For egg crack: hold close, thin score, let small sound lead the reaction.

## Where not to overapply / alternative interpretations
A purposeful camera/audio contradiction may signal unreliable perception, dream or comedy; document and test it.

## Observable acceptance test
Ask viewers at each cut: who heard/saw what, where source is, what motivated move.

## Typical failure
Cut to a zoomed frame and add loud whoosh without establishing object being tracked.
