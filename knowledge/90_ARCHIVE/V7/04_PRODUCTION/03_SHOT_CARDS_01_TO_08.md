# Film shot cards — every camera and sound choice justified by scene context

**24 proposed shot IDs A01–A24** adapted from V2, with V3 story-intent, performance, camera and sonic perspective. These are **planning cards**; no video has been rendered from them. Times may be revised after the 80s animatic; when revised update all frame event and audio data. A01 has mandatory full 270-frame specification in `02_BENCHMARK_270_FRAMES.md`.

## Scene 1 | Discovery, 0–9s | S01 | A01

**A01: One uninterrupted medium-wide full-body portal crossing, 0–9s.** Viewer needs certainty human emerged from aperture. Character checks the alien world before committing to step. Camera locks west-middle portal and visible floor; move is zero until after exit and preferably remains zero. Motion: portal opens with local foliage response; human head/hand inside, lead foot crosses and plants, pelvis/trailing leg clear, settle, eyes/head scan canopy. Sound: forest place → portal activation → threshold timbre → **actual event-matched** two foot plants → curious breath. **Primary attention: crossing geometry.** Reject edge entrance, floating portal, occlusion or foot-contact failure.

## Scene 2 | Rescue, 9–19s | S02

**A02: Forest/nest relationship, 9–12s.** Viewer question “What caught his attention?” Human first hears a remote stomp then spots a tall nest and vulnerable egg; eyes/head lead slow lean. Camera uses human eyeline to reveal nest EAST with consistent scale; slow reframe only after eye fixation. Sound: open ambience + distant offscreen mother footfall from right, subtly causing leaves/nest to vibrate. Primary = discovery of precarious egg. Reject unexplained egg motion or vague geography.

**A03: Egg close with danger, 12–14.5s.** Egg wobbles from delayed ledge vibration, center shifts beyond support, rolls toward lip. Camera close enough to see gravity and dangerous drop; nearby branch/ledge reference remains. Sound isolates egg/twig contact as larger distant thud decays. Primary = irreversible tipping. Reject egg launched without contact/weight cue or scale pop.

**A04: Continuous catch, 14.5–19s.** Human sees danger, bends knees, lunges, egg falls then touches palms, transfers momentum, human stumbles and catches balance. Camera medium/full-body holds egg, hands, feet and ground; no cut before/during contact. Sound: movement swish low priority, **contact-locked** hand catch, recovery scuff, second stronger approaching step after rescue. Primary = actual rescue and recoil. Reject egg teleport or hands attach precontact.

## Scene 3 | Mother's interpretation, 19–30s | S03

**A05: Giant foot arrives, 19–22s.** Audience learns what made thuds. Mother foot enters eastern frame and depresses ground; human orients right after vibration. Low camera with contextual ground/scale; a restrained physically caused camera settle follows impact, not a perpetual shake. Sound progresses from offscreen to near transient, ground crunch and leaves delayed. Primary = massive presence. Reject impact sound on a noncontact frame.

**A06: Searching mother's gaze, 22–25s.** Mother scans empty nest first, then locates egg in human's hands and changes posture. Use geography two-shot/insert as needed with east-right axis and eyeline matching; hold sufficient time for viewers to infer misunderstanding. Sound: searching breath, minimal music, subdued forest. Primary = eyes/knowledge state. Reject mouth roars before egg recognition.

**A07: Failed explanation, roar/recoil, 25–30s.** Human gestures toward nest with egg supported; mother narrows gaze, inhales, roars. Human recoils **after** roar begins, feet prepare turn. Camera medium two-shot shows both emotional states and scale; slight push toward dinosaur only if it does not hide human response. Sound: inhale → roar foreground → near human breath / controlled subjective narrowing; score ducks. Primary = misinterpretation and fear. Reject sound-first roar without mother reaction or human response-before-stimulus.

## Scene 4 | Protection and pursuit, 30–46s | S04

**A08: Decision to flee, 30–34s.** Human attempts to lower egg, sees mother closing, chooses chest cradle and westward escape. Show gaze → decision → hands retract → torso pivot → stance-foot push-off; do not cut these away. Camera shifts to readable western route with mother on right; source orientation intact. Sound: anxious breath, egg shift cloth, first accelerated contact steps, score pulse enters after choice. Primary = why he runs with egg. Reject instantaneous x-translation.

**A09: Chase geometry, 34–39s.** Mother accelerates and visibly reduces human-to-mother world-distance. Human runs west with planted contacts; mother strides heavy with tail counterbalance. Camera side-tracks at readable speed, displays both actor silhouettes and ground. Sound gait contact events (not loop clock), mother footfall mass, fast breath, music pulse with changing dynamics. Primary = closing threat. Reject dinosaur recedes while caption claims chase.

**A10: Branch obstacle, 39–43s.** Establish low branch in travel path BEFORE human reaches it. Human notices and ducks; mother's torso/head collides after human passes, branches break/displace paper fragments. Camera holds enough of same shot to preserve action topology; only modest follow-tracking. Sound branch rush from body motion, fracture AT contact, debris tail across subsequent moments. Primary = obstacle cause and physical collision. Reject branch materializing at impact or camera cut hiding contact.

**A11: Root stumble, 43–46s.** Human catches toe on root, pitches while egg stays cradled, executes recovery steps; mother draws closer. Camera slightly tighter only while keeping toe/root/egg and full action in composition. Sound toe scrape, startled gasp and recovery feet; music momentary rhythm disruption. Primary = near-failure. Reject detached egg, impossible recovery or heavy overcranked camera shake.

## Scene 5 | Hatching reverses pursuit, 46–56s | S05

**A12: Crack changes focus, 46–49s.** Crack from chest grabs human attention; human eyes drop, head then torso follow, running decelerates through real steps. Mother hears the first chirp later and starts braking. Camera track smoothly eases with actual actor slowdown; no immediate jump to locked pose. Sound: tiny crack/micro-rattle foreground; percussion retracts, breathing remains; a subjective narrowing is intentional. Primary = audience notices change in egg. Reject crack inaudible or premature stop.

**A13: Shell truly opens, 49–53.5s.** Crack topology grows, shell segments separate, baby head then body physically emerges while human supports weight. Camera insert now earned by action and not hiding its beginning; focus shell rim and small face. Sound small layered snaps, new airy inhalation, first chirp, no music masking. Primary = hatch action completed. Reject baby appearing full-sized in a single frame.

**A14: Shared recognition and mother stop, 53.5–56s.** Human and mother gaze converge at baby. Mother decelerates with leg plant and torso/tail carry-through. Camera medium two-shot locked to let emotion register, maintain gaze axis. Sound mother heavy skidding decays, small baby chirp and human breath; sparse underscore or quiet. Primary = changed emotional information. Reject dinosaur instant freeze or anger-to-friendliness without perception.

## Scene 6 | Trust, 56–66s | S06

**A15: Safe baby handoff, 56–60s.** Human looks to mother, decides to offer baby, crouches, lowers baby until feet touch, then releases hands. Camera medium-lower baby-level enough to show hands AND feet. Sound fabric/hand/ground paper Foley exactly contacts; music holds back. Primary = trust and careful release. Reject baby teleports or drops unsupported.

**A16: Baby to mother, 60–63s.** Baby looks up, starts unsteady steps, approaches mother's lowering snout; head and nose contact. Camera gentle baby-height medium preserving step path and looming parent. Sound light steps/chirps and mother warm breath, no oversized footsteps. Primary = approach with recognizable agency. Reject baby gliding or scale mismatches.

**A17: Mother's acceptance and bond seed, 63–66s.** Nuzzle completed; mother lifts soft gaze to human, he waves timidly, baby turns and chirps AT human. Camera held two-shot including all three, no unnecessary push-in; allow beat for viewer to infer respect and baby attachment. Sound soft inhale/exhale, short positive chirp, warm score enters after recognition. Primary = emotional resolution and plant for following. Reject music announcing forgiveness before faces/acting.

## Scene 7 | Child follows, mother notices late, 66–74s | S07

**A18: Return to portal and distraction, 66–69s.** Geography wide shows portal west, family east. Human waves and approaches portal; baby follows with eyes. Mother turns toward shell/nest debris briefly, having a plausible reason to miss the baby for a moment. Camera holds map, minimal pan; music returns small curious motif. Primary = space and motive established. Reject mother watching baby leave yet appearing surprised later.

**A19: Human truly crosses home, 69–71.2s.** Human enters threshold toward inside, limbs progressively disappear in spatially correct order; portal remains active. Camera holds complete threshold and feet, reusing accepted primitive from scene 1. Sound threshold timbre and beginning of interior room acoustics as a sound bridge. Primary = demonstrable exit from forest. Reject simple foreground graphic cover or actor vanishing off left edge.

**A20: Baby chooses and crosses, 71.2–73s.** Baby watches human vanish, glances at distracted mother, resolves to follow, chirps and runs through aperture with feet/plane masking. Camera follows little eye-trace but not so tight as to hide portal. Sound baby short purposeful chirp, quick tiny contacts and portal sound. Primary = intentional child choice. Reject unmotivated child teleport.

**A21: Mother discovers consequence, 73–74s.** Mother turns toward chirp, notices baby's absence, sights still-open portal and lunges toward it. Camera keeps portal direction known; breath/alarm after visual recognition, no gratuitous jump scare. Primary = mother pursuing her child. Reject mother pre-emptively moving to portal before noticing.

## Scene 8 | Comic intrusion, 74–80s | S08

**A22: Home crossing, 74–76s.** Human physically emerges through apartment portal, fully clears plane and plants foot on carpet. Baby crosses after, stumbles/lands safely. Camera medium with apartment/portal ground in frame; match threshold geometry and actor scale. Sound forest becomes dry interior; portal ring retains motif, two distinct contact transients. Primary = return home has actually happened. Reject person appearing in center of room without doorway.

**A23: Follower surprise / pressure, 76–78s.** Human sees baby, turns eyes, head and body in disbelief. Baby chirps happily. Behind them portal rim begins stretching from unseen force; portal tension sound can start slightly before visual deformation if established. Camera held to include human reaction and portal edge. Primary = surprise, followed by new threat. Reject instant camera zoom toward snout before viewer observes the human-baby reaction.

**A24: Mother's eye reveal / smash cut, 78–80s.** Huge snout presses **through** distorted aperture: part still on prehistoric side and part in room; rim deforms at contact. Eye comes into view. Human turns upward just in time. Camera may push slightly only after the snout's crossing proof; end with controlled sting and black frame/optional small end title without UI collision. Primary = comedic cliffhanger. Reject mother fully materializing, unexplained scale change or unreadable black/title timing.

## Editorial and cue handoff requirements

Every approved A## shot yields: `last frame/first frame image`, action event list, actor/properties at exit, exact camera side and screen direction, source sound position, audio bed/state, cut/bridge type and contact states. The next shot must inherit these values or declare an explicit visible story-time/location transition.
